Letting the scoring overrule a profitable backtest

Forecasting model vs. prediction markets · Python

I built a model that turns day-ahead temperature forecasts for 7 U.S. cities into probabilities, then compared them with Kalshi weather-market prices. The first paper backtest showed +7.7%, but the Brier score said the market was far better calibrated, so I reported the gain as luck rather than skill.

Across 4,433 settled markets, on the 1,216 markets the crowd was unsure about, the market's Brier score is 0.2114 and my model's is 0.3047. Paper ROI is -3.37% on 212 picks. The rule for real money has not been met, so none has been traded.
