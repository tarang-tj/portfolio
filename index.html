Independent project, 2026

Forecasting weather prediction markets

  1. My model turns day-ahead temperature forecasts for 7 U.S. cities into probabilities and compares them with Kalshi prices.
  2. The first paper backtest returned 7.7%.
  3. On the 1,216 contested markets, the market was better calibrated than my model (Brier 0.2114 vs 0.3047).
  4. I have not traded real money. My rule needs a better Brier score than the market on 200+ contested markets and a positive paper return, and neither is true yet.

SQL project, 2026

Segmenting online store customers in SQL

  1. A retail database with 620 orders from January 2024 to June 2025.
  2. I grouped the 189 buyers by how many orders they placed.
  3. Customers with four or more orders are a third of buyers.
  4. They account for 54.4% of revenue.
