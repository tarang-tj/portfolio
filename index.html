04 · SQL study, 620 orders

Who actually pays the bills in an online store

  1. 620 orders over 18 months. Who actually pays the bills?
  2. Split the 189 buyers by how often they come back.
  3. Loyal customers, four or more orders, are a third of buyers.
  4. They bring in 54.4% of the revenue.
Read the case →
ecommerce-sql / query 2.4 (excerpt)
CASE
  WHEN order_count = 1 THEN 'One-time buyer'
  WHEN order_count BETWEEN 2 AND 3 THEN 'Repeat buyer'
  ELSE 'Loyal customer (4+)'
END AS segment
segmentbuyersrevenue
One-time19.0%7.5%
Repeat47.6%38.1%
Loyal (4+)33.3%54.4%
