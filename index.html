Independent research, 2026

Testing a forecasting model against prediction markets

My model turns day-ahead temperature forecasts for 7 U.S. cities into probabilities and compares them with Kalshi prices. It has scored 4,433 settled markets.

The first paper backtest returned +7.7%. The calibration check further down is why I did not rely on it.

1,216 of them were contested, where the market itself was unsure.

On those, the market’s Brier score is 0.2114 and mine is 0.3047. Lower is better, so the market wins.

I have not traded real money. My rule needs a better Brier score than the market on 200+ contested markets and a positive paper return. Neither is true yet: paper return is -3.37% on 212 picks.

SQL project, 2026

Finding who pays the bills in an online store

A retail database with 620 orders from January 2024 to June 2025. 189 buyers placed at least one order that was not cancelled or refunded.

I grouped them by how many orders they placed: 36 bought once, 90 came back two or three times, and 63 placed four or more. That last group is a third of buyers.

ecommerce-sql / query 2.4 (excerpt)
CASE
  WHEN order_count = 1 THEN 'One-time buyer'
  WHEN order_count BETWEEN 2 AND 3 THEN 'Repeat buyer'
  ELSE 'Loyal customer (4+)'
END AS segment

They bring in 54.4% of revenue.
