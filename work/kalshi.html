Forecasting weather prediction markets

A temperature forecasting model scored against 4,433 Kalshi markets.

The model turns day-ahead temperature forecasts for 7 U.S. cities into probabilities and compares them with Kalshi market prices.

The first paper backtest returned 7.7%. The Brier score, which measures calibration, showed the market was much better, so I did not treat the return as evidence the model works.

What I found

Across 4,433 settled markets, on the 1,216 where the market was unsure, the market scores 0.2114 and my model scores 0.3047. Paper return is -3.37% on 212 picks. My rule for real money has not been met, so none has been traded.
