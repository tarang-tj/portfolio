Quadcore Innovations, 2025 to 2026

Cutting support tickets at Quadcore

Tracing most support tickets to one crash path and getting it fixed.

support tickets, before and after

Resolution time: 2 to 4 days before, 24 hours after.

My role
Operations Improvement Analyst
Context
Fintech payments platform
Timeline
Oct 2025 to May 2026
Tools
SQL, Power BI, Tableau

I mapped the platform with the people who operate it and traced about 70% of support tickets to a single crash path. The product lead accepted the fixes I proposed.

I also replaced a manual weekly reporting cycle with automated SQL and Power BI feeds behind four Tableau dashboards. Over the same period, monthly active users grew from 400 to 550.

What I found

Tickets fell from about 100 to 40, and resolution time went from 2 to 4 days to 24 hours.
