Who actually pays the bills in an online store

A third of buyers bring in more than half the revenue.

ecommerce-sql / query 2.4
CASE
  WHEN order_count = 1 THEN 'One-time buyer'
  WHEN order_count BETWEEN 2 AND 3 THEN 'Repeat buyer'
  ELSE 'Loyal customer (4+)'
END AS segment

A six-table retail schema with 620 orders and $112,344 in sales, analyzed with cohort and window-function queries.

The segment query splits buyers by how often they come back, then compares each group’s share of buyers with its share of revenue.

What I found

Loyal customers, with four or more orders, are a third of buyers (63 of 189) and bring in 54.4% of revenue. One-time buyers are 19% of buyers and 7.5% of revenue.
